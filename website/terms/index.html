A few useful expectations

Terms of use

Mingle is pre-release software for local development with coding agents. These brief notes describe how to use it responsibly.

Pre-release availability

Features may change, be incomplete, or be temporarily unavailable. Keep backups of important projects and review changes before relying on them.

You direct your work

You are responsible for the projects and accounts you connect, the requests you send to a selected provider, and reviewing agent output before using it. Provider services have their own terms and privacy practices.

Remote work needs explicit approval

Connecting an account or device, accepting a project invitation, publishing an agent, or viewing device presence does not by itself authorize an agent run, command, Git push, or merge on another device. Remote actions require the applicable explicit approval from the device owner; push and merge remain separate actions.

Use projects you are allowed to access

Only connect repositories, accounts, devices, and provider services you are authorized to use, and follow the applicable terms for those services.
